Leveling With the Gods

Fan-made reading sample · Mythic Fantasy / Action

Synopsis

A fallen challenger returns to a world shaped by ancient powers and impossible trials. Every new level offers strength, but the greatest test is deciding what that strength should serve.

Chapter 1: The Trial of Embers

The altar stood at the center of a ruined temple, surrounded by statues whose faces had been deliberately erased. A message burned above it: power will be granted to the one who survives the trial, not the one who defeats every opponent.

Dae studied the others gathered in the chamber. Each carried a different weapon and a different reason to win, but all of them watched the glowing altar as if it were the only thing that mattered. When the floor opened, they fell into a maze lit by blue fire.

Instead of racing toward the prize, Dae followed the echoes of people calling for help. He reached the altar late, covered in dust and carrying two injured rivals. The flame accepted him anyway. The trial had been measuring the kind of person he became while chasing power.
