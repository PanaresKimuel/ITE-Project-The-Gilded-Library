Leviathan

Fan-made reading sample · Oceanic Horror / Survival

Synopsis

In a world threatened by enormous creatures from the deep, survivors struggle to protect what remains of their communities. Courage means making hard choices when safety is never guaranteed.

Chapter 1: The Signal Below

The fishing boat’s sonar returned a shape too large to fit on the screen. Captain Sera ordered the engines cut and listened to the hull groan as something passed beneath them, blotting out the weak light of morning.

Their island settlement had survived storms and shortages, but the sea had become unpredictable. Sera’s crew carried medicine and water for a neighboring village, and turning back would leave people waiting without supplies. She studied the waves, the fuel gauge, and the frightened faces around her.

They followed the coast instead of the direct route, using the cliffs as cover and the old lighthouse as a guide. The journey took twice as long, and the creature’s shadow followed them until dusk. When they reached shore, the villagers met them with lanterns, proving that survival was not only about escaping danger but keeping promises across it.

This fan-made sample is an original piece created for a student website project; it is not an official chapter of Leviathan.
