Nano Machine

Fan-made reading sample · Martial Arts / Science Fantasy

Synopsis

A martial artist receives access to advanced technology in a world governed by ancient rivalries. Innovation changes the balance of power, but judgment determines whether progress becomes protection or domination.

Chapter 1: A Needle of Light

The device appeared as a silver needle no longer than a finger, its surface marked by lines that moved like living script. It promised to analyze the body, correct flaws, and calculate the perfect strike.

Seon tested it in the training hall and watched his movements sharpen with every repetition. The other students noticed immediately. Some asked for help; others accused him of cheating, and the instructors demanded to know who had given him such a tool.

When a sparring partner collapsed from overtraining, the device recommended a faster regimen. Seon shut it off and called for the physician instead. A tool could measure motion and predict outcomes, but it could not decide when ambition had become dangerous.

This fan-made sample is an original piece created for a student website project; it is not an official chapter of Nano Machine.
